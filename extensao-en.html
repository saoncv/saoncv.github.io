University Extension & Technological Outreach

1. SPE Unicamp Student Chapter (Faculty Advisor & Co-Sponsor)

Faculty Advisor and Co-Sponsor for the SPE UNICAMP Student Chapter. Founded in 1989 as the pioneer Society of Petroleum Engineers organization in Brazil, the chapter promotes technical workshops, short courses, career mentorship, and industry integration events, internationally recognized with the SPE Outstanding Student Chapter Award.

Visit SPE Unicamp Chapter ↗

2. Projeto Radiante — FEM / UNICAMP (Faculty Advisor & Co-Sponsor)

Academic advisorship and co-sponsorship for Projeto Radiante at UNICAMP's School of Mechanical Engineering. The initiative develops extension technology projects focused on sustainability, energy transition, social innovation, and hands-on engineering education.

Visit Projeto Radiante ↗

3. Technological Outreach & Knowledge Transfer

Development of technology dissemination programs on flow assurance, virtual sensing, and hybrid intelligence for the energy industry, alongside Open Science and patenting initiatives (Petrobras / Unicamp Inventor Award).
