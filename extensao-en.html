Outreach Projects, Technology Transfer & Innovation

1. SPE Unicamp Student Chapter (Faculty Advisor & Co-Sponsor)

Serving as Faculty Advisor and Co-Sponsor of the SPE UNICAMP Student Chapter. Founded in 1989 as the pioneer Society of Petroleum Engineers student organization in Brazil, the chapter conducts technical workshops, career mentorship, and industry networking events, earning the international Outstanding Student Chapter Award.

Visit SPE Unicamp ↗

2. Projeto Radiante — FEM / UNICAMP (Faculty Advisor & Co-Sponsor)

Academic mentorship and sponsorship of Projeto Radiante at the School of Mechanical Engineering (FEM / UNICAMP). The initiative develops technological outreach projects focused on sustainability, energy transition, social-environmental innovation, and hands-on engineering education.

Visit Projeto Radiante ↗

3. Technology Transfer, Entrepreneurship & Intellectual Property

Knowledge dissemination, patent licensing for the energy industry, and entrepreneurship fostering in partnership with the Inova Unicamp Innovation Agency and Petrobras. Patented technologies include non-intrusive multiphase flow measurement via structural vibration and hybrid intelligence algorithms.
