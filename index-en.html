Saon Crispim Vieira

Assistant Professor | Energy Department (FEM / UNICAMP)

Former Senior Petroleum Engineer & Technical Consultant at Petrobras (2008–2026)

Assistant Professor at the Energy Department of UNICAMP (FEM) and former Senior Petroleum Engineer / Technical Consultant at Petrobras (2008–2026). With a multidisciplinary background (B.Sc. in Control and Automation Engineering, M.Sc. in Petroleum Engineering, and Ph.D. in Thermal and Fluids from UNICAMP), he has extensive industrial experience in offshore R&D.

His research focuses on the convergence of data science and physics. He is the founder of PHI Lab (Physics-informed Hybrid Intelligence), leading projects in Multiphase Flow, Flow Assurance, and Physics-Informed Machine Learning for developing virtual sensors and optimized hybrid models. He is a recognized inventor with patented technologies (INPI/USPTO) applied to production monitoring, awarded the Petrobras Inventor and Unicamp Inventor Awards.
