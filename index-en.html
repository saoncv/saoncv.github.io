Research Axes

Slug Flow Dynamics & Intermittent Flow

Experimental characterization and mathematical modeling of gas-liquid intermittent slug flow in horizontal and inclined pipes. Two-state Markov chain modeling for hydrodynamic pressure fluctuation signatures.

Artificial Lift & Gas-Liquid Separation

Hydrodynamic flow field modeling inside centrifugal gas-liquid separators and Electrical Submersible Pump (ESP/BCSS) skids for subsea boosting and flow assurance.

Non-Intrusive Instrumentation & FSI

Development of wall-integrated multi-capacitance arrays and piezoelectric sensors for non-intrusive flow pattern identification, void fraction estimation, and flow-induced vibration monitoring.

Physics-Informed Machine Learning

Integrating first-principles fluid dynamics with deep learning for real-time virtual sensing, digital twins of oil wells, and intelligent production monitoring.
