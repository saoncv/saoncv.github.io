Saon Crispim Vieira

Assistant Professor | Energy Department (FEM / UNICAMP)

Former Senior Petroleum Engineer & Consultant at Petrobras (2008–2026)

Assistant Professor at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Energy Department at UNICAMP since 2026. Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering focused on Thermal & Fluid Sciences (2022), M.Sc. in Petroleum Science and Engineering (2018), and B.Sc. in Control and Automation Engineering (2007) from UNICAMP, with executive specializations in Financial Economics (UNICAMP, 2017) and Petroleum Engineering (Petrobras, 2009). Worked for 18 years at Petróleo Brasileiro S.A. – Petrobras (2008–2026) as Senior Petroleum Engineer and Technical Consultant in digital transformation for offshore production monitoring.

Extensive industrial R&D experience in the energy sector, participating in the conception, deployment, and operation of major offshore production projects. Leads research initiatives in Multiphase Flows, Artificial Lift Systems, Flow Assurance, and Subsea Boosting Systems. Current research interests integrate technological development with data science, investigating Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI) and Physics-Informed Machine Learning with differentiable models for virtual sensors, hybrid physics-AI modeling, and real-time optimization of complex production systems. Inventor of patented technologies (INPI/USPTO) for multiphase flows, recognized with the Petrobras Inventor Award and Unicamp Inventors Award.

Research Axes

Slug Flow Dynamics & Intermittent Flow

Experimental characterization and mathematical modeling of gas-liquid intermittent slug flow in horizontal and inclined pipes. Two-state Markov chain modeling for hydrodynamic pressure fluctuation signatures.

Artificial Lift & Gas-Liquid Separation

Hydrodynamic flow field modeling inside centrifugal gas-liquid separators and Electrical Submersible Pump (ESP/BCSS) skids for subsea boosting and flow assurance.

Non-Intrusive Instrumentation & FSI

Development of wall-integrated multi-capacitance arrays and piezoelectric sensors for non-intrusive flow pattern identification, void fraction estimation, and flow-induced vibration monitoring.

Physics-Informed Machine Learning

Integrating first-principles fluid dynamics with deep learning for real-time virtual sensing, digital twins of oil wells, and intelligent production monitoring.
