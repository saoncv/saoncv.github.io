Assistant Professor at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Energy Department at UNICAMP since 2026. Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering focused on Thermal & Fluid Sciences (2022), M.Sc. in Petroleum Science and Engineering (2018), and B.Sc. in Control and Automation Engineering (2007) from UNICAMP, with executive specializations in Financial Economics (UNICAMP, 2017) and Petroleum Engineering (Petrobras, 2009). Worked for 18 years at Petróleo Brasileiro S.A. – Petrobras (2008–2026) as Senior Petroleum Engineer and Technical Consultant in digital transformation for offshore production monitoring.

Extensive industrial R&D experience in the energy sector, participating in the conception, deployment, and operation of major offshore production projects. Founder and head of the PHI Lab — Laboratory for Physics-informed Hybrid Intelligence (associated with ALFA Lab / CEPETRO / FEM / UNICAMP), leading research initiatives in Multiphase Flows, Artificial Lift Systems, Flow Assurance, and Subsea Boosting Systems. Current research interests integrate technological development with data science, investigating Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI) and Physics-Informed Machine Learning with differentiable models for virtual sensors, hybrid physics-AI modeling, and real-time optimization of complex production systems. Inventor of patented technologies (INPI/USPTO) for multiphase flows, recognized with the Petrobras Inventor Award and Unicamp Inventors Award.

Research Axes & Strategic Focus

1. Multiphase Flows, Transients & Non-Linear Dynamics

Physical characterization and hydrodynamic modeling of gas-liquid and liquid-liquid multiphase flows under steady-state and transient regimes. Investigation of hydrodynamic stability, intermittent and chaotic dynamics (slug flow), flow pattern transitions, and stochastic modeling (Markov chains, spectral analysis) in horizontal and inclined pipes.

$$\frac{\partial (\rho_m \mathbf{u}_m)}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho_m \mathbf{u}_m \mathbf{u}_m) = -\nabla p + \nabla \cdot \boldsymbol{\tau} + \rho_m \mathbf{g} + \mathbf{F}_{\text{transient}}$$

2. Physics-Informed Hybrid Intelligence, Soft Sensing & Instrumentation

Integration of first-principles fluid dynamics with Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PINNs, neural operators, and surrogate models). Development of virtual sensors (soft sensing), non-intrusive instrumentation (wall pressure sensors, vibration, and capacitance), data-driven methods, and digital twins for real-time inference and monitoring.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{PHI}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{data}} + \lambda_1 \|\mathcal{N}_{\text{physics}}(\hat{u})\|^2 + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{boundary}}$$

3. Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI)

Theoretical, experimental, and numerical investigation of the dynamic coupling between internal multiphase flows and the structural vibrational response of flexible and rigid pipelines. Analysis of flexural waves, pressure-fluctuation-induced fatigue, and aero/hydroelastic coupling in industrial piping systems.

$$EI \frac{\partial^4 w(x,t)}{\partial x^4} + m_{\text{pipe}} \frac{\partial^2 w(x,t)}{\partial t^2} = F_{\text{fluid-FSI}}(x,t;\,\Delta p, \alpha)$$

4. Petroleum Engineering, Artificial Lift & Flow Assurance

Industrial R&D applications for the energy sector and offshore production systems. Modeling and optimization of Artificial Lift systems (ESP/BCSS, Gas Lift), centrifugal multiphase separation in skids, severe flow flow assurance mitigation, and integrated oilfield production data analytics.

$$\nabla \cdot (\alpha_k \rho_k \mathbf{u}_k) = \Gamma_k, \quad \Delta P_{\text{total}} = \Delta P_{\text{friction}} + \Delta P_{\text{hydrostatic}} + \Delta P_{\text{acceleration}}$$
