Undergraduate & Graduate Teaching (FEM / UNICAMP)

Academic faculty teaching at the Energy Department of the School of Mechanical Engineering (FEM / UNICAMP) covering Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer, and Thermal Engineering for Mechanical Engineering (10G) and Control & Automation Engineering (49G).

⚙️ B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering (Major 10G / FEM / UNICAMP)

Courses taught / cataloged in the official DAC catalog:
• EM360 — Thermodynamics I: Fundamental concepts, 1st and 2nd Laws, mass and energy analysis for closed and open systems.
• EM460 — Thermodynamics II: Gas/vapor power cycles, refrigeration cycles, psychrometrics, and equations of state.
• EM461 — Fluid Mechanics I: Fluid statics, integral conservation equations, dimensional analysis, and incompressible viscous duct flows.
• EM561 — Fluid Mechanics II: Navier-Stokes equations, potential flow, boundary layer theory, compressible flows, and turbulence.
• EM570 — Heat Transfer I: Steady/transient 1D and multidimensional heat conduction and thermal radiation.
• EM670 — Heat Transfer II: Internal/external forced and natural convection, heat exchangers (LMTD and NTU methods).

🤖 B.Sc. in Control and Automation Engineering (Major 49G / FEM / UNICAMP)

Courses taught / cataloged in the official DAC catalog:
• ES460 — Thermal Engineering I: Fundamentals of applied thermodynamics, working fluid properties, and thermal-fluid analysis for mechatronic systems.
• ES560 — Thermal Engineering II: Applied fluid mechanics, conduction/convection/radiation heat transfer, heat exchangers, and industrial thermal integration.

🎓 Graduate Studies (PPGEM-FEM / PPG-CEP / UNICAMP)

Advanced Courses & Research Topics:
• Multiphase Flow & Advanced Thermal-Fluids: Hydrodynamic modeling of gas-liquid and liquid-liquid multiphase flows, flow patterns, Drift-Flux and two-fluid models, stability, and transient phenomena for M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs at UNICAMP.
