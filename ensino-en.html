Academic Teaching & Courses

Prof. Dr. Saon Crispim Vieira — PHI Lab / DE / FEM / UNICAMP

Undergraduate and Graduate courses offered at the Energy Department of the School of Mechanical Engineering (FEM / UNICAMP).

Courses & Academic Teaching (FEM / UNICAMP)

1. Thermal Engineering I & II (Undergraduate)

Fundamentals of applied thermodynamics, power cycles, refrigeration cycles, and heat transfer for Control & Automation Engineering and Mechanical Engineering students at UNICAMP.

2. Fluid Mechanics I & II (Undergraduate)

Fluid statics and dynamics, integral and differential conservation equations (Navier-Stokes), dimensional analysis, viscous incompressible flows, and introduction to turbulence for B.Sc. Mechanical Engineering.

3. Multiphase Flow & Advanced Thermal-Fluids (Graduate)

Hydrodynamic modeling of gas-liquid and liquid-liquid multiphase flows, flow patterns, Drift-Flux and two-fluid models, stability, and transient phenomena for M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs at UNICAMP.
