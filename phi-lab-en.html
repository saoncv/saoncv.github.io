Identity & The Meaning of PHI (φ)

Physics-informed Hybrid Intelligence represents more than an acronym: it is a scientific philosophy.

The symbol φ (phi) carries profound significance in mathematics, physics, and fluid mechanics, representing void fraction, porosity, phase variables, scalar fields, potential functions, level set methods, and continuous differentiation. It also evokes the mathematical elegance of the golden ratio, symbolizing harmony between physical knowledge, learning, and computation.

The Five Principles of PHI

  • 01 Physics before Data: Data enriches models; Physics gives them meaning.
  • 02 Everything is Differentiable: Simulations, numerical operators, and learning share the same gradient flow.
  • 03 Learning Augments Physics: Learning accelerates, calibrates, and complements physics without replacing it.
  • 04 Reality Closes the Loop: Experiments are part of the learning process itself.
  • 05 Engineering is the Benchmark: Success is measured by the ability to solve real-world problems.
